## Quick Reference

### Authenticity Scoring Framework (0-100)

| Score Range | Authenticity Level | Characteristics                     | Action Required                 |
| ----------- | ------------------ | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------- |
| **91-100**  | Excellent          | Highly authentic, human-like        | Ready for publication           |
| **71-90**   | Good               | Mostly authentic, minor AI patterns | Light improvements recommended  |
| **51-70**   | Fair               | Mixed human/AI characteristics      | Significant improvements needed |
| **31-50**   | Poor               | Predominantly AI-like               | Major revisions required        |
| **0-30**    | Very Poor          | Clearly AI-generated                | Complete rewrite needed         |

### AI Pattern Detection Indicators

1. **Sentence Length Consistency** - AI often uses uniform sentence lengths
2. **Vocabulary Complexity** - AI may use overly complex or uniformly simple vocabulary
3. **Emotional Pattern Analysis** - AI struggles with nuanced emotional expression
4. **Paragraph Structure Regularity** - AI often creates similarly structured paragraphs
5. **Topic Transition Logic** - AI transitions may be formulaic
6. **Specificity Level Analysis** - AI often lacks specific, unique details
7. **Error Pattern Recognition** - Characteristic AI mistakes vs. human errors
8. **Style Consistency Overload** - Perfect consistency indicates AI generation

### SEO Structure Validation Checklist

1. **Title Tag Optimization** - Primary keyword presence, appropriate length
2. **Header Structure** - H1-H6 hierarchy, keyword distribution
3. **Meta Description** - Compelling, keyword-rich, optimal length
4. **Internal Linking** - Relevant links, appropriate anchor text
5. **Content Length** - Comprehensive coverage, topic authority
6. **Keyword Density** - Natural integration, not stuffing
7. **Readability** - Clear language, proper formatting
8. **Mobile Optimization** - Responsive design, readable on small screens
9. **Page Speed Considerations** - Content structure affects loading
10. **Schema Markup** - Structured data for search engines
