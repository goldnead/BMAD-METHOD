## Quick Reference

### Interview Angle Framework

1. **Expert Journey Angle** - Professional background and evolution
2. **Problem-Solution Angle** - Challenges overcome and innovations developed
3. **Industry Insight Angle** - Behind-the-scenes knowledge and predictions
4. **Human Impact Angle** - Personal stories and emotional connections
5. **Future Vision Angle** - Trends, predictions, and forward-looking insights
6. **Contrarian View Angle** - Unconventional perspectives or controversial opinions

### Question Generation Matrix

| Question Type            | Purpose                 | Example Framework                           |
| ------------------------ | ----------------------- | ------------------------------------------- |
| **Origin Questions**     | Establish background    | "How did you first get involved with...?"   |
| **Process Questions**    | Understand methodology  | "Walk me through your thinking when..."     |
| **Impact Questions**     | Explore consequences    | "What changed when you discovered...?"      |
| **Future Questions**     | Anticipate developments | "Where do you see this heading in 5 years?" |
| **Story Questions**      | Elicit narratives       | "Tell me about a time when..."              |
| **Contrarian Questions** | Challenge assumptions   | "What do most people get wrong about...?"   |

### Trust Building Techniques

- **Mirroring** - Reflect expert's communication style
- **Vulnerability** - Share appropriate personal insights
- **Validation** - Acknowledge expertise and contributions
- **Curiosity** - Show genuine interest beyond the agenda
- **Listening** - Use silence and follow-ups effectively

### Story Discovery Patterns

- **Transition Moments** - Career changes, breakthroughs, failures
- **Emotional Peaks** - Moments of pride, excitement, or struggle
- **Learning Experiences** - What shaped their expertise
- **Human Connections** - Family, mentors, teams that influenced them
- **Future Dreams** - What drives them forward
