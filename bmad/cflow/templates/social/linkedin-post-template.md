# LinkedIn Post Templates

**Platform:** LinkedIn
**Formats:** Mini-Post (text-only) & Carousel (multi-slide)
**Purpose:** Professional thought leadership, B2B engagement
**Tone:** Professional yet conversational, value-driven

---

## LinkedIn Mini-Post (Text-Only)

**Character Limit:** 3,000 characters (optimal: 1,300-1,500 for engagement)
**Format:** Text with optional 1 image or document
**Best Times:** Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9am or 12-1pm
**Goals:** Engagement (comments, shares), thought leadership

---

### Mini-Post Structure

#### Hook (Line 1-2)

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll, make them click "see more"

**Formats:**

**Pattern 1: Contrarian Statement**

```
Most [people in industry] are doing [thing] wrong.

Here's what actually works:
```

**Pattern 2: Personal Story Opening**

```
Last week, a client told me:
"[Frustration/pain point]"

I knew exactly what the problem was.
```

**Pattern 3: Curiosity Gap**

```
I spent [time] analyzing [thing].

The results surprised me.
```

**Pattern 4: Data Hook**

```
[Statistic] of [audience] struggle with [problem].

I used to be one of them.
```

**Examples:**

```
❌ "Content marketing is important for growth."
✅ "I deleted 60% of my content last month. Traffic went UP."

❌ "Here are some tips for better emails."
✅ "My newsletter gets 47% open rates. Here's the exact template I use."
```

---

#### Body (Main Content)

**Length:** 800-1,200 characters
**Structure:** Problem → Insight → Solution/Framework

**Format Options:**

**Option A: Numbered List (High Engagement)**

```
Here's what I learned:

1. [Insight #1]
   → [Brief elaboration]

2. [Insight #2]
   → [Brief elaboration]

3. [Insight #3]
   → [Brief elaboration]

4. [Insight #4]
   → [Brief elaboration]

5. [Insight #5]
   → [Brief elaboration]
```

**Option B: Story Arc**

```
[Setup: The situation]

[Conflict: The problem/challenge]

[Insight: The realization]

[Resolution: What changed]

[Lesson: The takeaway]
```

**Option C: Before/After**

```
Before I figured this out:
• [Pain point 1]
• [Pain point 2]
• [Pain point 3]

After implementing this system:
• [Result 1]
• [Result 2]
• [Result 3]

The difference? [Key insight]
```

**Option D: Framework Share**

```
Here's the [X-Step] framework:

→ Step 1: [Name]
[One sentence explanation]

→ Step 2: [Name]
[One sentence explanation]

→ Step 3: [Name]
[One sentence explanation]

It's simple, but it works.
```

---

#### Close & CTA

**Length:** 100-200 characters

**Format Options:**

**Engagement CTA (Comments):**

```
What's been your experience with [topic]?
Drop a comment - I read every one.
```

**Value CTA (Link in Comments):**

```
I wrote a complete guide on this.
Dropping the link in the comments 👇
```

**Thought Provocation:**

```
[Provocative question that invites debate]

Curious what you think.
```

**Community Building:**

```
If this resonated, follow me for more [niche] insights.
I post weekly.
```

**Examples:**

```
✅ "What's your biggest struggle with [topic]? Let's discuss."
✅ "I put together a template for this. Link in comments if you want it."
✅ "Agree? Disagree? I want to hear both sides."
❌ "Click the link in my bio!" (too salesy)
```

---

#### P.S. (Optional)

**Use for:**

- Link to resource
- Related content mention
- Behind-the-scenes note

**Example:**

```
P.S. I went deep on this topic in my newsletter this week.
Link in comments if you're subscribed.
```

### LinkedIn Mini-Post Template (Full Example)

```markdown
Most coaches think they need MORE content.

They're wrong.

I just deleted 60% of my blog content.
Here's what happened:

• Organic traffic: +40%
• Time on page: +3 minutes
• Email signups: +25%

The reason? I cut the noise and kept only content that:

1. Addresses a specific pain point
   → Not generic "how to" posts

2. Has unique insights from my experience
   → Not regurgitated advice from competitors

3. Leads to a clear next step
   → Not just "thanks for reading"

Less content. Better content. Bigger results.

---

The full analysis (with screenshots) is in my newsletter.
Link in comments if you want the breakdown 👇

What's your approach - more content or better content?
```

**Formatting Tips:**

- Use line breaks liberally (every 1-2 sentences)
- Use emojis sparingly (1-3 per post max)
- Use bullet points for lists
- Use "→" for sub-points or elaboration
- Use "---" to separate sections visually

## LinkedIn Carousel Post (Multi-Slide)

**Slide Count:** 5-10 slides (optimal: 7 slides)
**Dimensions:** 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
**Format:** PDF or image carousel
**Engagement:** Higher reach than single-image posts

---

- High contrast (dark text on light background or vice versa)
- Consistent design across slides
- Use icons or simple graphics to support (not distract)

### Design Specifications

**Carousel Images:**

- Format: PDF (best) or PNG/JPG
- Dimensions: 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1350px (portrait)
- Max file size: 10MB (PDF), 5MB (images)
- Recommended: 5-10 slides
- Design tool: Canva, Figma, Adobe

**Design Guidelines:**

- Consistent branding (colors, fonts)
- High contrast for readability
- Mobile-first design (most viewers on mobile)
- Minimal text per slide
- Professional but not corporate

## Best Practices

**Engagement Optimization:**

- Post during business hours (7-9am, 12-1pm, 5-6pm local time)
- Tuesday-Thursday perform best
- First comment with link (LinkedIn algorithm prefers this)
- Respond to every comment within first hour
- Use 3-5 relevant hashtags (at end of post)

**Content Strategy:**

- Mix formats: 70% mini-posts, 30% carousels
- Share personal stories and contrarian takes
- Be specific with numbers and examples
- End with questions to drive comments
- Repurpose best-performing newsletter/blog content

**What Works on LinkedIn:**

- Personal experience and stories
- Contrarian or surprising insights
- Actionable frameworks and templates
- Behind-the-scenes and lessons learned
- Data-backed observations

**What Doesn't Work:**

- Generic motivational quotes
- Overly promotional content
- Long blocks of text without breaks
- Corporate jargon and buzzwords
- Clickbait without substance

## Hashtag Strategy

**Use 3-5 hashtags per post, placed at the end**

**Format:**

```
[Post content]

---

#ContentMarketing #B2BMarketing #LinkedInTips #CreatorEconomy #Entrepreneurship
```

**Hashtag Types:**

- Industry-specific (1-2): #ContentMarketing, #B2BMarketing
- Niche-specific (1-2): #NewsletterGrowth, #SEOStrategy
- Broad reach (1): #Entrepreneurship, #Leadership

**Find effective hashtags:**

- Use LinkedIn's search to check follower counts
- Target: 10K-100K followers (sweet spot)
- Avoid: Generic mega-hashtags (>1M followers)
- Mix: Niche + Broad
