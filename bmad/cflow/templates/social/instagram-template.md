# Instagram Templates

**Platform:** Instagram
**Formats:** Reel (video), Carousel (multi-slide), Single Image (quote/visual)
**Purpose:** Visual storytelling, brand awareness, community building
**Tone:** Casual, authentic, visually-driven

---

## Instagram Reel Template

**Duration:** 7-90 seconds (optimal: 15-30 seconds)
**Dimensions:** 1080x1920px (9:16 vertical)
**Format:** MP4, H.264 codec
**Best Times:** 11am-2pm, 7-9pm
**Goals:** Reach, engagement, trend participation

---

### Reel Structure

#### Hook (First 3 Seconds) - CRITICAL!

**Purpose:** Stop the scroll immediately

**Hook Formats:**

**Text Overlay Hooks:**

```
"Everyone does [thing] wrong"
"Stop doing [thing]"
"[Number] signs you're [situation]"
"POV: You just discovered [thing]"
"The [thing] no one talks about"
"This changed everything for me"
```

**Visual Hooks:**

- Unexpected transformation (before/after)
- Bold statement or question
- Pattern interrupt (unusual visual)
- Relatable situation acting

**Examples:**

```
✅ "I spent $10K learning this. Sharing it for free:"
✅ "Your content isn't bad. You're just missing this:"
✅ "POV: You finally understand [concept]"
❌ "Hey guys, today I'm going to show you..." (too slow)
```

---

**Purpose:** Deliver value quickly

**Content Formats:**


**Format 2: Before/After Transformation**

```

[Show problem state with text overlay]

After:
[Show solution state]

[Key insight text overlay]
```

**Format 3: Process/Tutorial**

```
Step 1: [Action]
(Show in action)

Step 2: [Action]
(Show in action)

Step 3: [Action]
(Show result)
```

**Format 4: Storytime**

```

(Dramatic music/visual)

(Uplifting music/visual)
```

---

#### CTA (Last 3-5 Seconds)

**Purpose:** Drive action (save, share, follow, comment)

**CTA Formats:**

```
"Save this for later →"
"Share with someone who needs this"
"Follow for more [niche] tips"
"Double tap if you agree"
"Comment [word] for the template"
"Link in bio for full guide"
```

**Visual CTA:**

- End screen with your handle
- "Follow for more" text overlay
- Question to answer in comments
- Direction to link in bio

### Reel Technical Specs

**Video:**

- Resolution: 1080x1920px (9:16)
- Frame rate: 30fps
- Duration: 7-90 seconds (optimal 15-30s)
- Format: MP4, H.264 codec
- Max file size: 4GB

**Audio:**

- Use trending audio when relevant
- Add voiceover for educational content
- Background music: energetic but not overpowering
- Volume: -6dB to -12dB for mixing with voiceover

**Text Overlays:**

- Large, bold font (min 60pt)
- High contrast (white text on dark background or vice versa)
- Max 2 lines of text per frame
- 1-3 seconds per text overlay
- Positioned in "safe zone" (center 80% of frame)

## Hashtag Strategy for Instagram

**Use 10-30 hashtags per post**

**Hashtag Mix:**

- Mega (1M+ posts): 3-5 hashtags
- Large (100K-1M posts): 5-8 hashtags
- Medium (10K-100K posts): 7-10 hashtags
- Niche (<10K posts): 5-10 hashtags

**Placement:**

- At end of caption after "•••" line breaks
- OR in first comment (some prefer for cleaner look)

**Example Set (Content Marketing):**

```
Mega: #ContentCreator #ContentMarketing #SocialMediaMarketing #Entrepreneurship #DigitalMarketing
Large: #ContentStrategy #CreatorEconomy #SocialMediaTips #MarketingTips #ContentCreation
Medium: #ContentCreators #NewsletterGrowth #ContentWriting #MarketingStrategy #SoloPreneur
```

## Best Practices

**Posting Frequency:**

- Reels: 3-5x per week
- Carousels: 2-3x per week
- Quote posts: 1-2x per week
- Mix content types

**Optimal Post Times:**

- Best: 11am-1pm, 7-9pm (local time)
- Test your audience's engagement patterns
- Consistency > perfect timing

**Engagement Tactics:**

- Respond to comments within first hour
- Use "comment [word] for [resource]" to boost engagement
- Ask questions in captions
- Use polls and questions in Stories
- Tag relevant accounts (max 5)

**Content Strategy:**

- 80% value, 20% promotional
- Batch create content (1 month at a time)
- Repurpose blog/newsletter content
- Save high-performing content for re-posts (after 3-6 months)

**What Works on Instagram:**

- Authentic, behind-the-scenes content
- Educational value (how-to, tips, frameworks)
- Relatable humor and storytelling
- Visual appeal (clean design, good lighting)
- Trending audio (for Reels)

**What Doesn't Work:**

- Over-polished, corporate content
- Long captions with no breaks
- Generic stock photos
- Over-promotional posts
- Ignoring trends completely
