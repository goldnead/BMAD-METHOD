# Facebook Post Template

**Platform:** Facebook
**Formats:** Story Post (long-form), Image Post, Video Post, Link Post
**Character Limit:** 63,206 characters (but optimal is much shorter)
**Purpose:** Community engagement, storytelling, longer-form content
**Tone:** Conversational, warm, community-oriented

---

## Facebook Story Post (Long-Form)

**Optimal Length:** 200-600 words (1,000-3,000 characters)
**Format:** Text-focused with optional image
**Best For:** Personal stories, vulnerable shares, detailed insights

---

### Story Post Structure

#### Opening Hook (First 2-3 Lines)

**Purpose:** Grab attention before "See More" cutoff

**Critical:** First ~130 characters are visible before "See More" - make them count!

**Hook Formats:**

**Format 1: Vulnerable Opening**

```
I almost quit [thing] last month.

Then something changed.

[See More]
```

**Format 2: Unexpected Confession**

```
Unpopular opinion:

[Controversial but honest statement]

Here's why I believe this:
```

**Format 3: Relatable Problem**

```
Can we talk about [frustrating thing]?

Because I'm tired of pretending it's not an issue.
```

**Format 4: Story Tease**

```
Last week, [dramatic event].

It taught me something I wish I'd known years ago.
```

**Format 5: Question Hook**

```
Have you ever [relatable situation]?

That was me last Tuesday. Let me tell you what happened...
```

---

#### Story Body (Main Content)

**Length:** 400-1,200 words
**Structure:** Setup → Conflict → Resolution → Lesson

**Storytelling Guidelines:**

**1. Be Specific**

```
✅ "I was sitting in my car outside the Starbucks on Main Street, crying into my steering wheel at 2pm on a Tuesday."

❌ "I was feeling down one day."
```

**2. Use Dialogue**

```
✅ My coach said, "You know what your problem is? You're trying to be perfect instead of being YOU."

That hit me like a truck.
```

**3. Show Emotion**

```
✅ "My hands were shaking as I hit 'publish.' What if they hated it? What if I looked stupid?"

❌ "I was nervous to publish."
```

**4. Build Tension**

```
✅ "Three months went by. Nothing. Zero sales. $5,000 spent on ads. I started updating my resume."

❌ "It didn't work at first but then it did."
```

**5. Include the Transformation**

```
✅ "Then I changed ONE thing. Not my offer. Not my pricing. My messaging.

Sales went from 0 to 7 in 10 days."

❌ "Then I figured it out and things got better."
```

---

#### The Lesson (Key Takeaway)

**Purpose:** Tie the story to actionable insight

**Format:**

```
Here's what I learned:

[1-3 key insights with elaboration]

Maybe this resonates with you. Maybe it doesn't.

But if you're [experiencing related problem], I hope this helps.
```

**Example:**

```
Here's what I learned:

Authenticity isn't about being raw 24/7.

It's about being honest about the journey - including the parts that aren't Instagram-perfect.

My audience didn't want a guru. They wanted a human.

When I showed up as ME - messy, imperfect, figuring it out - that's when everything changed.

If you're holding back because you think you need to have it all together first...

You don't. Show up anyway.
```

---

#### Closing & Engagement

**Purpose:** Invite conversation

**CTA Formats:**

**Format 1: Vulnerable Ask**

```
Anyone else been here?

Or is it just me being dramatic again? 😅
```

**Format 2: Open Question**

```
I'd love to hear your take.

What's been your experience with [topic]?
```

**Format 3: Community Check**

```
If this resonated, drop a ❤️ in the comments.

If you've got a story, I'm all ears.
```

**Format 4: Resource Offer**

```
I wrote a full guide on this (link in comments).

But honestly, just knowing you're not alone in this is sometimes enough.
```

## Facebook Image/Visual Post

**Image Dimensions:** 1200x630px (recommended)
**Text Overlay:** Minimal (20% rule no longer enforced but less is more)
**Caption Length:** 100-300 characters (shorter for image posts)

---

### Image Post Format

**Caption Structure:**

```
[Hook - 1-2 lines]

[Context or insight - 1-2 lines]

[CTA - comment, share, or tag]
```

**Example:**

```
Your content isn't bad.

Your distribution is.

Tag someone who needs to hear this 👇

[Image shows distribution framework graphic]
```

---

## Facebook Link Post

**Purpose:** Drive traffic to blog/newsletter/resource

**Important:** Facebook deprioritizes link posts - use strategically!

---

### Link Post Format

**Caption Structure:**

```
[Hook that makes them want to click]

[2-3 lines explaining what they'll get]

[CTA to click link]

[Link URL]
```

**Best Practices:**

- Write compelling caption (don't rely on auto-preview)
- Explain WHY they should click
- Use link in first comment (gets better reach than in post)
- Post as image with link in comments (alternative)

**Example:**

```
I just published the most honest thing I've ever written about content creation.

It's about why following "best practices" made my content worse.
And the counterintuitive approach that 3x'ed my engagement.

If you're tired of cookie-cutter content advice, this is for you.

Full article in the comments 👇

(Or find it on my profile - latest post)
```

## Facebook Video Post

**Video Length:** 1-3 minutes (optimal)
**Dimensions:** 1080x1080px (square) or 1080x1920px (vertical)
**Auto-play:** First 3 seconds are critical!

---

### Video Post Structure

**First 3 Seconds:**

- Bold text overlay with hook
- Visual pattern interrupt
- Must work without sound (80% watch muted)

**Video Content:**

- Talking head (builds connection)
- B-roll with text overlays
- Screen share tutorials
- Behind-the-scenes

**Caption:**

```
[Hook]

[What's in the video]

[CTA to watch/comment]

---

[Optional: Longer context in caption for those who want more]
```

## Engagement Best Practices

### Timing

**Best Post Times:**

- 9am-10am (morning coffee scroll)
- 12pm-1pm (lunch break)
- 7pm-9pm (evening wind-down)

**Best Days:**

- Wednesday, Thursday (highest engagement)
- Sunday (good for longer stories)
- Avoid: Monday morning, Friday evening

### Facebook Algorithm Tips

**The Algorithm Favors:**

- Posts that spark conversations (comments)
- Content people spend time reading (long captions)
- Native content (not links)
- Video content (especially live video)
- Posts with high early engagement (first 30 min)

**Boost Engagement:**

- End with open-ended questions
- Respond to every comment in first hour
- Use emojis sparingly (1-3 per post)
- Tag relevant people/pages (max 3)
- Post in Facebook Groups you're active in

## Content Strategy for Facebook

### Content Mix (Weekly)

**2-3 Story Posts:**

- Personal stories
- Vulnerable shares
- Lessons learned
- Behind-the-scenes

**1-2 Value Posts:**

- Tips/insights
- How-to content
- Framework shares

**1 Link Post:**

- Blog article
- Newsletter
- Resource/guide

**1-2 Engagement Posts:**

- Questions
- Polls
- "This or that"

---

### What Works on Facebook

**✅ Authentic, personal stories**

- Vulnerability resonates
- Real experiences over generic advice
- Show the human behind the brand

**✅ Longer-form content**

- Facebook users read more than other platforms
- Depth builds trust and authority
- Story format performs well

**✅ Community-building content**

- Questions that spark discussion
- Content that invites sharing
- Posts that create connection

**✅ Native video and images**

- Video gets 1.5-3x the reach of text
- Images with personal photos (not stock)

---

### What Doesn't Work

**❌ Over-promotional content**

- "Buy my course" posts without value
- Too many sales pitches
- Link dumping

**❌ Copy-paste from other platforms**

- X threads posted as-is (wrong format)
- LinkedIn corporate-speak
- Instagram hashtag spam

**❌ Clickbait without substance**

- "You won't believe..." titles
- Empty promises
- Manipulation tactics

**❌ Impersonal, corporate tone**

- Press release style
- Jargon-heavy language
- Lack of personality

**Use sparingly:**

- Niche-specific only

## Repurposing from Other Platforms

### From Blog/Newsletter to Facebook

**Adapt:**

- Make it more personal and story-driven
- Add emotional context
- Expand on the human elements
- Remove formal structure

### From X Thread to Facebook

**Transform:**

- Convert thread into flowing narrative
- Add more detail and context
- Expand tweets into full paragraphs
- Keep conversational tone but less punchy

### From LinkedIn to Facebook

**Adjust:**

- Make it less professional, more personal
- Add more vulnerability
- Use warmer, friendlier tone
- Focus on story over authority

## Formatting Tips

### Line Breaks

```
Use line breaks generously.

They make long posts readable.

Double breaks between major sections.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
Use dividers for visual separation.
```

### Emojis

```
Use emojis naturally (not forced):

✅ "Anyone else? 😅"
✅ "Here's what I learned 👇"
❌ "🔥💯 This is the BEST content strategy 🚀💪"
```

### Paragraphs

```
Keep paragraphs short (2-4 sentences).

It's easier to read on mobile.

Long blocks of text = instant scroll-past.
```

## Measurement & Optimization

**Track:**

- Engagement rate (comments + reactions + shares / reach)
- Story posts vs. value posts performance
- Best performing topics
- Optimal posting times for YOUR audience

**Optimize:**

- Double down on high-engagement topics
- Test different story formats
- Vary post length
- Experiment with video vs. text

**Monthly Review:**

- Top 3 performing posts
- Common themes in high engagement
- Adjust content mix based on data
